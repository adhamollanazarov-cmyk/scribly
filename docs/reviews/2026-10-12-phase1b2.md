## Should positive be required?

**Yes for directed components, signed currents, torque/oriented quantities and other
results whose sign encodes a reference direction; no for every numerically signed calc.**
Negative potential energy, work, scalar charge and ordinary potential are scalars.
Magnitudes have no direction of their own. Requiring an arbitrary direction for those
would invent semantics and trigger unnecessary paid fixes.

Use an explicit draft quantity kind (for example scalar, magnitude, component,
oriented quantity) and frame/reference ID. Require a canonical nonempty `positive`
for components/oriented quantities even when the value is zero: the frame remains
meaningful, but `physical(0, positive)` correctly returns None. Zero should match an
expected zero without pretending it points in a direction. Require empty `positive`
for scalars/magnitudes. Do not infer this from negativity or units: N may describe a
force component or its magnitude, and J may describe signed scalar work. Apply schema,
prompt, fixture and evaluation updates together if this proposal is accepted; it is
not implemented in this highs-only change. Alias `up -> +y` also presumes the physics
frame; it must not silently override a problem that defines positive y downward or
be confused with SVG screen y.
