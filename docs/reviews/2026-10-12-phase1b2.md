## Should positive be required?

**Yes for directed components, signed currents, torque/oriented quantities and other
results whose sign encodes a reference direction; no for every numerically signed calc.**
Negative potential energy, work, scalar charge and ordinary potential are scalars.
Magnitudes have no direction of their own. Requiring an arbitrary direction for those
would invent semantics and trigger unnecessary paid fixes.

Use an explicit draft quantity kind (for example scalar, magnitude, component,
oriented quantity) and frame/reference ID. Require a canonical nonempty `positive`
for components/oriented quantities even when the value is zero: the frame remains
meaningful, but `physical(0, positive)` correctly returns None. Zero should match an
expected zero without pretending it points in a direction. Require empty `positive`
for scalars/magnitudes. Do not infer this from negativity or units: N may describe a
force component or its magnitude, and J may describe signed scalar work. The authorized
follow-up below implements this proposal with prompt, draft, fixture and evaluation
updates together. Alias `up -> +y` also presumes the physics
frame; it must not silently override a problem that defines positive y downward or
be confused with SVG screen y.

## Authorized follow-up — 2026-10-12

- **M1:** Required `quantity_kind` and `frame` in CalcDraft; component/oriented results
  require readable nonempty `positive`, even at zero. Scalars/magnitudes require empty
  frame/direction; magnitudes reject negative expected values. Invalid shape or persistent
  sign failures cannot finish a verified lesson after the existing one fix.
- **M2:** Convention annotations are tracked by canonical direction and frame, matched
  before use (an earlier step, or at=0 before result annotations). Unrelated axes, junk
  labels, different frames, reversed Cartesian arrows and late annotations are rejected.
  Explicit frames may choose independent conventions. Physics +y is up in SVG coordinates.
- **M3:** Calc literals and values no longer authorize raw physical numbers. Givens and
  unit-bearing examples explicitly declared in narration/board text provide provenance;
  fractional formula coefficients remain allowed only in formulas. This lexical lint
  does not prove every quantity's physical provenance or dimensional consistency.
- **M4:** Unit macros require spacing or a unit wrapper. `2\Omega` and `2\mu` remain
  symbols; `100\,\Omega`, `2\,\mu\text{C}` and wrapped units remain supported.
- **M5:** Expected physical directions are validated at construction/load and matched
  fail-closed, even for invalid unvalidated objects. Directed matches require component/
  oriented ledger metadata and a frame. Expected zero has empty physical direction and
  can match a zero component, whose reference convention remains declared. Grader v4
  marks older scores as incomparable; existing live reports were not re-scored.
- **M6:** Battery charging problem now explicitly asks for ideal-emf power, excluding
  its internal resistor. The independent answer remains -9.133109619686818 W.
- **L1/L2:** Added PyYAML stubs, separated problem reasons and aggregate fix counters,
  included script mypy in AGENTS §6, and rejected nonfinite physical-direction inputs.
- **M7 remains intentionally unfixed:** Prose/result labels/arrows can contradict correct
  metadata. The Phase 3 visual QA loop must compare rendered steps with narration;
  named/projected geometry also requires visual review. No new manual gate was added.
